# 💖 Happy Birthday Madhulu! (03/11/2026) 🎂🎈

A magical, interactive birthday and love celebration web application created specially for **Madhulu**.

---

## 🎶 "I Wanna Be Yours" Background Song & Local Import
- **Default Playback:** Automatically plays a synthesized atmospheric arrangement of the iconic Arctic Monkeys' *"I Wanna Be Yours"* chord progression (Cm → Ab → Fm → Gm).
- **Import Your Local Song:** Click the **"📂 Import MP3"** button in the top navigation bar to select your local MP3/audio file of *"I Wanna Be Yours"* directly from your computer!
  - It saves in your browser's local cache (IndexedDB) so it stays loaded every time you open the site.
  - Or simply place your file at `assets/audio/i-wanna-be-yours.mp3`.

---

## 🌟 The 5 Chapters of Love for Madhulu

### 1. 💘 Shoot Cupid's Arrow into the Heart
- Target: A pulsing ruby crystal heart.
- Pull back Cupid's bow and release, or click **"Fire Cupid's Arrow at Heart!"**.
- Pierces the heart, splitting it open to reveal:
  > *"DIRECT HIT! Happy Birthday, My Beloved Madhulu! You stole my heart the day we met, Madhulu, and every single beat belongs to you."*

### 2. 🎂 Madhulu's Animated Layered Birthday Cake
- 3D-styled designer cake assembled step-by-step:
  - Base: Rich Belgian Truffle
  - Middle: Strawberry Velvet Cream
  - Top: Vanilla Dream with Fresh Berries
  - Topper: Golden *"✨ Happy Birthday Madhulu ✨"* banner
  - 5 striped candles with realistic flickering flames.
- Tap the candles or click **"Blow Out All Candles for Madhulu! 🌬️"** to make a wish!

### 3. 🎈 5 Floating Surprise Balloons & Love Compliments
- 5 floating helium balloons in the night sky.
- Click each balloon to pop it and reveal 5 love sentences crafted specially for Madhulu:
  1. *"Madhulu, your radiant smile lights up my whole universe and effortlessly melts away every sorrow."*
  2. *"Your pure heart, softness, and kindness make you the most beautiful person I have ever known, Madhulu."*
  3. *"Your voice and laughter are my favorite melody in this world — nothing brings me more peace than being with you."*
  4. *"You inspire me every single day with your grace, strength, and the breathtaking way you love."*
  5. *"Madhulu, you are my forever home, my safest shelter, and my greatest blessing. I Love You endlessly."*

### 4. 📸 Our Cherished Moments (Attach Madhulu's Photos)
- Polaroid memory board with date: **03/11/2026**.
- **Ready for Madhulu's Photos:** Click **"➕ Attach / Upload Madhulu's Photos"** to pick multiple photos from your device at once!
- Tap any polaroid to view it enlarged with next/previous lightbox controls.

### 5. 💌 A Letter to Madhulu Along With "I Love You"
- A parchment love letter dated **03/11/2026**:
  > *"My Dearest Madhulu... Just like the song playing in our hearts, I wanna be yours — today, tomorrow, and through all of eternity."*
- Grand interactive wax seal button:
  > **❤️ I LOVE YOU, MADHULU ❤️**
- Clicking the seal triggers a heartbeat sound, rising floating love hearts, and a fireworks celebration!
